How to play

Hold the screen to charge the power ring, release to hop. Land in the center of a platform to grow your combo. Go as far as you can!

My purchases are missing

Tap Settings › Restore purchases in the Game while signed in with the Apple Account (iOS) or Google account (Android) you bought them with.

Ads

"Remove Ads" removes interstitial ads. You can change ad personalisation under Settings › Privacy options.
